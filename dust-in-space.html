Dust In Space — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026
Developer: AloneLab
App identifier: com.alonelab.dustinspace

Overview

This policy explains how AloneLab handles information in the Android and iOS store releases of Dust In Space. The game does not require an AloneLab account or sign-in.

Information handled by the game

The game stores chapter progress and preferences, such as sound, vibration and reduced-motion settings, locally on your device. AloneLab does not receive these saved files. The store release does not send gameplay analytics, advertising identifiers, location data or diagnostic reports to AloneLab, and does not include advertising or tracking services.

The game plays audio and may use vibration. It does not record audio or access your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Purchases and app stores

If you purchase content through Google Play or the Apple App Store, the relevant store processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. AloneLab does not receive your payment card details. Store services may process purchase information to provide, verify or restore your purchase. Any purchase integration that changes how AloneLab receives or handles information will be reflected in this policy.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for information about their services.

Contacting support

If you email AloneLab, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request and resolve support issues, and retain it only as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords or payment card details. Email is handled through our email service provider.

Sharing

AloneLab does not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Support correspondence may be processed by the email provider that delivers and stores it, or disclosed when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Local game progress can be deleted using Reset Progress in the game's options. To remove all locally stored game data, use your device's app-data controls or uninstall the app. This does not cancel purchases or delete transaction records held by an app store. Contact the relevant store for its purchase-record and account controls.

You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations. There is no AloneLab game account to delete.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to AloneLab through support correspondence, contact us so we can address it.

This policy webpage

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it may cause GitHub to process technical information, such as your IP address, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This page contains no analytics scripts, advertising or embedded tracking services added by AloneLab.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game or our practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.
